Setu (सेतु)

Teacher-Controlled Adaptive Learning for Middle-School Maths

What Setu Solves

"A wrong answer is a starting point, not a diagnosis. Setu checks a possible prerequisite gap, offers a focused lesson when the evidence supports it, and guides the student back to practice. Teachers can review the evidence and intervene."

In middle-school classrooms where devices are shared, students often struggle with advanced topics due to unaddressed foundational prerequisites. Rather than endlessly repeating high-grade questions, Setu steps back through an authored concept graph to probe prerequisite mastery, delivers focused 2-minute micro-lessons, administers bridge re-checks, and smoothly returns the student to practice.

The 4-Beat Learning Journey

  1. Wrong Answer on Practice: Student makes an error on an original concept (e.g. sign transposition in linear equations).
  2. Prerequisite Diagnostic Probe: Engine evaluates foundational concepts without penalizing practice counters.
  3. Focused Micro-Lesson: When probe evidence supports a gap, a concise bridge lesson is presented.
  4. Bridge Re-Check & Return: Verification question confirms understanding; upon passing, practice resumes on the original topic.

Teacher Command & Shared Devices

Teachers retain full pedagogical command. The teacher dashboard clusters students by shared prerequisite gap for recommended 10-minute small-group clinics, highlights thin/conflicting evidence cases, and enables logged teacher overrides. For low-connectivity classrooms, Setu functions offline via service workers and local caching, while paper-based practice can be entered through Paper Mode.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Setu?

Setu is an adaptive learning system designed for middle-school mathematics on shared classroom devices, turning student errors into diagnostic hypotheses.

How does adaptivity work?

It probes prerequisite concepts across an authored knowledge graph, suggests focused bridge lessons when supported by evidence, and tracks mastery.

What data does Setu store?

Zero direct PII: pseudonymous IDs only, salted PIN hashes, and cryptographic tombstones for device deletion synchronization.

What are the limitations of this build?

Content is an authored Class 8 CBSE Linear Equations spine; BKT estimates are uncalibrated and hand-set; content is author-drafted and unreviewed; demo controls only.
